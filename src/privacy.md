# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

MachXDynamics is a flow simulation for iPhone. It runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains what that means in practice.

This policy covers three things: the MachXDynamics app, this website (machxdynamics.com), and our social media profiles. They are handled separately below.

---

## The app

### What the app collects

**Nothing.**

The simulation is computed on your device. The app makes no network connections of its own, has no sign-in, and contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs. Your settings and slider positions never leave your device. We never see your name, email address, IP address or any identifier we could tie to you. iOS may contact Apple's App Store to confirm how the app was installed; this is handled by Apple and sends nothing to us.

### What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few small values on your device using iOS's standard app storage (UserDefaults): for example, whether you have seen the first-launch hint, and a one-time measurement of how fast the simulation runs on your iPhone. These values stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. They are included in your own iCloud or device backup only if you have turned that on for your device; that backup is held by Apple, not by us.

### Crash reports

The app itself sends no crash reports or diagnostics. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple — not the app — collects crash logs under Apple's own privacy policy and may make anonymous crash statistics available to us. They contain nothing we can link to you, and we use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in the iOS setting above.

### Downloads and purchases

Downloads and any purchases go through Apple's App Store. Apple processes them independently of us, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We see only aggregated, anonymous figures in App Store Connect. We never see who you are or how you paid.

Some App Store links we publish, on this website and on our social media profiles, carry a campaign label (for example, `ct=MXD-web`). The label is the same for everyone who uses that link. Apple's App Analytics then shows us aggregated figures per label, such as product page views and first-time downloads, and holds back very small groups. We never see who clicked or downloaded, and the app itself sends nothing.

## This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no embedded content from third parties. The site uses your device's built-in fonts; nothing is loaded from a third-party font service.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari may show a banner at the top of this site that links to the app on the App Store. Safari loads the banner's details from Apple; this site sends nothing to Apple for it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. This is necessary to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website.

GitHub may process this data in the United States. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub keeps these logs is governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); we have no access to them.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed for that purpose, unless statutory retention periods apply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

## Our social media profiles

We run profiles for MachXDynamics on YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.) and Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). Our website only links to them; nothing from these platforms is embedded here, and nothing is loaded from them until you follow a link. When you visit a profile, the platform processes your data under its own privacy policy, which may include transfers to the United States. We have no control over that processing.

The platforms show us aggregated statistics, such as views and watch time, and we see what you post publicly or send us there. We use this to present the app and answer questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We do not use advertising pixels or upload contact lists.

For Instagram page statistics, Meta and we may be joint controllers under Art. 26 GDPR. Meta's Page Controller Addendum sets out the responsibilities; you can also send requests directly to Meta.

## Your rights under the GDPR

The app collects no personal data, so there is nothing for the usual rights to act on. For the limited data described under "This website" and "Our social media profiles" and for emails you send us, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To use them, write to the contact address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live.
