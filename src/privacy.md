# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

MachXDynamics is a flow simulation for iPhone. It runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains what that means in practice.

This policy covers two things: the MachXDynamics app, and this website (machxdynamics.com). They are handled separately below.

---

## The app

### What the app collects

**Nothing.**

The simulation is computed on your device. The app makes no network connections of its own, has no sign-in, and contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs. Your settings and slider positions never leave your device. We never see your name, email address, IP address or any identifier we could tie to you. iOS may contact Apple's App Store to confirm how the app was installed; this is handled by Apple and sends nothing to us.

### What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few small values on your device using iOS's standard app storage (UserDefaults): for example, whether you have seen the first-launch hint, and a one-time measurement of how fast the simulation runs on your iPhone. These values stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. They are included in your own iCloud or device backup only if you have turned that on for your device; that backup is held by Apple, not by us.

### Crash reports

The app itself sends no crash reports or diagnostics. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple — not the app — collects crash logs under Apple's own privacy policy and may make anonymous crash statistics available to us. They contain nothing we can link to you, and we use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in the iOS setting above.

### Downloads and purchases

Downloads and any purchases go through Apple's App Store. Apple processes them independently of us, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We see only aggregated, anonymous figures in App Store Connect. We never see who you are or how you paid.

## Your rights under the GDPR

The app collects no personal data, so there is nothing for the usual rights to act on. For the limited data described under "This website" and for emails you send us, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To use them, write to the contact address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live.
